About me

I studied physics and gained experience in data engineering. Today, my focus is on artificial intelligence and automation, two fields where I can combine my desire to understand how things work with my drive to build solutions based on that understanding. For me, programming is also a way to explore my interests, work through new problems, and turn ideas into concrete projects.

In my personal projects, this curiosity takes different forms: I develop apps and tools, modify video games to explore their possibilities, and pursue independent mathematical research, as in the Ringmin project. I also teach mathematics and physics, which allows me to share what I have learned with future generations.

My goal is to never stop learning, put ideas to the test, and create something accessible and open source that can be useful to others.
