# Master home with the Nexus skin (direction V3)

- Direction V3 replaces V2's "Master home & technical sheets". The home keeps the master structure and its hero animation, takes the polish of Nexus Portfolio (header bar, identity card, project cards with colour covers) and holds every project. The technical sheets, the plate numbers and the separate projects page are gone. Every section opens and closes and no list scrolls endlessly. The approved look is the V3 prototype of 2026-11-23 (`falker47-v3-prototype.zip`); the build renders it statically, in both languages.
- Audience and first task: in one screen, recruiters, colleagues and users understand who Maurizio is (AI Engineer; software, modding, research, tutoring) and reach any project in at most 2 interactions (hero action or header link → card link).
- Tokens: hero band #0a1424 with a 34 px grid (rgba(155,220,255,.035) lines), only inside the band; below it the page surface is #0f1014, cards #171a21, raised controls #1f232c; header bar #0c0d11 at 93% opacity; rules rgba(255,255,255,.08). Text #f1f4f5, secondary #c9d7e2, muted #adbfce (card text #d8dee9, card meta #a9b2c0); accent #9bdcff; Nexus blue #60a5fa for the role and focus rings; violet #a78bfa in gradients. Category colours on borders and labels only: app #60a5fa, games #f0a35e, tools #4fd1a5, mods #c084fc. Social Viewer keeps its product accent #49c7bb on its own pages.
- Type, self-hosted woff2 latin: Gelasio 400 and 400 italic for the hero title, tabs, intros, big counts and paper titles (metric-compatible with Georgia, so the hero keeps its metrics); IBM Plex Mono 400 for labels, stacks, counts lines and numbers in drawings; Poppins 600 for the name, the role, the brand, the header links, card titles, pills and card buttons; system sans for body. 4 px spacing grid. 16 px card radius; 999 px radius for the primary CTA and the pills.
- Heading scale outside the hero: page title (h1) ≤ 40 px; every other heading ≤ 32 px.
- Header, on every page: a full-width sticky bar, 64 px including its 1 px bottom rule: brand · Progetti · Ricerca · Chi sono (EN: Projects · Research · About) · IT/EN flags. The three links open `#progetti` on the Software tab, `#progetti` on the Research tab (`#ricerca`) and `#chi-sono`. At ≤ 699 px the links sit behind a menu button (a disclosure: `aria-expanded`, `aria-controls`, Escape, outside click); without scripts they stay visible as a second row.
- Hero: its own full-bleed band with the grid, three soft radial glows (blue, violet, teal) that drift over 28 s, a fade into the page at its foot and a 1 px bottom rule. The title, the CTA, the identity card and the pulse diagram keep the positions and sizes of V2 and F3. The italic half of the title carries the blue-violet text gradient. The identity card has a 22 px radius, a 1 px gradient ring (#60a5fa66 → #ffffff10 → #a78bfa33), an inner surface rgba(18,21,28,.82) with backdrop blur, the name in Poppins 600 and the role in 11 px uppercase with .14em tracking, #60a5fa (one line from 600 px; two lines on phones, where the card grows by that line).
- Motion: the hero pulse once per load; the 28 s glow drift; the disclosure height animation; 150 ms opacity on tab change; one pulse along the "Percorso" path; card lift on hover. All off with prefers-reduced-motion.
- Glow and gradients: allowed for the hero band, the title gradient, the brand name, the identity card ring and shadow, the primary CTA shadow, card hover, the "Percorso" pulse, the download bar and the headers of translation cards and paper figures. Elsewhere surfaces are flat.
- Forbidden: emoji, video, register rows, technical-sheet frames and plate numbers, more than one primary CTA per view, CDNs, third-party requests (the Nexus snapshot on `main` is the only external fetch).
- Home "/": header → hero (single CTA "Esplora i miei progetti" → `#progetti`; identity card with GitHub, LinkedIn, Email as `#contatti`) → collapsible "Chi sono" (`#chi-sono`) → collapsible "I miei progetti" (`#progetti`) → one-line signature footer. Deep links `/#progetti`, `/#ricerca`, `/#chi-sono` and `/#certificazioni` open their section on load; the page scrolls once the opening animation has ended and stops 76 px below the top.
- "Chi sono": two text columns → "Percorso", a line drawing in the hero diagram style between two rules (wide with four nodes joined by a wave from 900 px, tall below: FISICA, CREDITO, DATI, AI & INNOVAZIONE, the last one highlighted) → the philosophy line → the profile row (Ricerca → `/research/`) → `#certificazioni`, "Certificazioni · 11": a fan of 11 overlapping cards (150 px, 112 px on phones, −9° to +9°). A card lifts on hover or focus and the caption beside the fan names it; any card opens the viewer, a `<dialog>` with the title, the full image, "← n / 11 →", arrow keys, swipe, Escape and focus return.
- "I miei progetti": four tabs.
  - Software: the three counts (app · giochi · strumenti, computed from `data/projects.json`) are the category toggles: a tile with its icon, the count and the label (`aria-pressed`, App first, no "All"), beside "App · 4 progetti · scorri per vederli tutti". Below, a horizontal scroll-snap row of cards with 44 px ← → buttons under it (hidden on phones): about 3.15 cards per view on desktop, 1.6 on tablets, 86% of the row on phones. A card: 16:9 cover, title, kind pill and stack, summary, link buttons (Apri, Play Store, Prova l'app, GitHub, in data order). The title opens the project's own page when there is one.
  - Modding: the toggles "N mod" and "N traduzioni italiane" over every published mod from `data/nexus-mods.json`, beside "N download unici · N pubblicazioni · dati Nexus del <date>". A card: the mod's picture (a typographic "IT" header with the game for translations), name, game pill and category, downloads in large type, endorsements, a thin bar of width √(downloads / max) and "Nexus ↗".
  - Ricerca: the paper count (→ `/research/`), then two cards with the graphic on top. Paper I shows the twelve certified configurations from `data/ringmin.json` (6 × 2, four columns on phones); Paper II charts R(n)/n² for n = 3…18 with the 1/8 line. A footer line explains both graphics and links Power-Ringmin and Zenodo.
  - Lezioni: the two rating cards and the lessons card.
  - All groups are rendered at build time. Scripts only show one tab and one category at a time, so the content exists without them.
- Project pages (`/research/`, `/projects/prof-gecko/`, `/projects/camaleonte/`, `/projects/social-viewer/` with `test/` and `privacy/`, `/uses/`): shared header; breadcrumb "← I miei progetti" → `/#progetti` ("← Home" on `/uses/`, "← Social Viewer" on its two subpages); one card with the cover, a label, h1, lead, three facts (Tipo / Tecnologie / Dove) and the links; then short blocks under ruled headings. The Social Viewer privacy page keeps the original text byte for byte.
- `/projects/` is a redirect stub to `/#progetti`. Shared footer on the pages: name, handle, Email, GitHub, LinkedIn, ORCID and "Strumenti che uso" (`/uses/`).
- Languages: IT rendered by default, EN through the flags on the same URL. Every visible string and accessible label exists in both.
- Data: `data/projects.json`, `data/mods.json` (covers and repositories of the three featured mods), `data/nexus-mods.json` and `data/nexus-downloads.json` (written by the Nexus refresh), `data/ringmin.json` (written by `scripts/import-ringmin.mjs`), `data/research.json`, `data/certifications.json`, `data/tutoring.json`, `data/i18n/{it,en}.json`. Counts are always computed, never typed.
