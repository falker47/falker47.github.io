# Working on falker47.github.io

The static site builds with Node 22 and its standard library. Run commands from the checkout root.

## Routes and data

`src/routes.mjs` declares the public pages. Directory routes build to `index.html` and `/404.html` supplies the error document. Shared head, header, flag switch and footer live in `src/partials/`.

| Pages | Routes |
| --- | --- |
| Home, research and recommendations | `/`, `/research/`, `/uses/` |
| Project details | `/projects/prof-gecko/`, `/projects/camaleonte/`, `/projects/social-viewer/` |
| Social Viewer testing and privacy | `/projects/social-viewer/test/`, `/projects/social-viewer/privacy/` |
| Redirect stub | `/projects/` → `/#progetti` |

The home is the master home with the Nexus skin (direction V3, [design brief](docs/unify/BRIEF.md); the approved look is the V3 prototype, kept outside the repository at `~/sources/v3-prototype`): the header bar (three home sections, then "Cosa uso" at `/uses/`), the hero in its own band, the collapsible "Su di me" (`#chi-sono`, certificates at `#certificazioni`) and "I miei progetti" (`#progetti`, four tabs; `#ricerca` is its Research tab), and a one-line footer. Each section row shows its title in Gelasio, a mono line of what it holds counted from the data (under the title on phones) and a plus drawn in a circle; the whole row lights up on hover and focus. Every project lives in the home: `src/pages/home/work.mjs` renders the software categories from `data/projects.json` in data order ("App", "Giochi", "Strumenti"), every published mod from `data/nexus-mods.json` (the three covers and repositories in `data/mods.json` win over the Nexus pictures), the two papers from `data/research.json` as project cards in a rail like the other tabs (the drawings of `src/partials/ringmin.mjs` as covers, "Paper I/II" and the arXiv number in the meta line, the result as the summary; the second line stays on `/research/`), and the tutoring cards from `data/tutoring.json` (side by side from 900 px). A project with its own page shows "Scheda →" on its cover, and its title link spreads over the cover. `src/pages/home/about.mjs` renders five rows, each paragraph in Gelasio under the mono name of its chapter (Studi, Percorso, Lavoro, Metodo, Tempo libero) and followed by its figure (on its right from 900 px; from 1200 px the text keeps one column of about 60 characters); the work paragraph is split in two (the path with the certificate fan, then the work and the method). With the script the rows are the slides of a carousel, one paragraph at a time in reading order, all as tall as the tallest so the controls never move: the chapters, one named segment per paragraph and each a button to it (the names only for screen readers on phones), then "Indietro" and "Avanti" (in the accent) at the two ends of the text column and a "1 / 5" counter between them; on the last paragraph "Avanti" becomes "I miei progetti" and opens that section, and the arrow keys step while the focus is on the controls; a link to `#certificazioni` brings its slide in. Without the script the rows stay in a column. Under the carousel come only the profile links, each with the mark of the place it opens: LinkedIn, GitHub, OEIS and ORCID in their colours, the Letuelezioni logo for the lessons, the Ringmin circles for `/research/`. The figures: for the studies a drawing in the hero line language (`src/partials/about-studies.mjs`: a simple pendulum under α = −(g/L) sin θ, then the same law as a semi-implicit Euler step in Python and the undamped θ(t) it computes; tests pin the formula, the code and the constant amplitude), for the path the certificate fan, for the work and method a drawing in the same language (`src/partials/about-method.mjs`: a "dati.csv" window whose eight scattered points two tangles cross, then the points on axes under one straight line, y = a x + b, each tied to it by its residual), then tiles counted from the data at build time, two to a row, each a link to its section: for the way of working the public GitHub repositories (`githubRepositories` in `data/profile.json`, the count GitHub shows, updated by hand) beside the Nexus unique downloads, then the three software categories; for the rest of the time the arXiv papers and the tutoring rating, with a card of the students' reviews from `data/tutoring.json` under them (the portal and its stars, short excerpts quoted in Italian from Letuelezioni signed with the student's name, month and year, advanced every six seconds and still on hover, focus and reduced motion, and dots marking the open one). Each glyph is a badge like the Nexus emblem (the category emblems, a phone with its apps, a wrench over a screwdriver and a gamepad, white on squares of the category colours the cards use, the star white on amber, the GitHub mark on GitHub's dark, the official arXiv logomark on a light one). The rating is the mean of `data/tutoring.json` ratings weighted by their number (`src/lib/rating.mjs`). The certificate fan (`certificateDeck` in `src/partials/components.mjs`) from `data/certifications.json`; its caption names the main issuers and shows the count large, with an italic hint. Every count in the three panels (the category toggles, the Nexus total, the paper count) is one tile shaped like the "Su di me" figures; the software toggles wear the same category emblems (dimmed until pressed), the paper count the arXiv logomark on a light square, the Modding toggles a line drawing; the pressed toggle is outlined in the panel accent. The card buttons are the foot of each card: up to three in a row, sharing its width, the platform mark left of the label; a card too narrow for three gives the first a row of its own. The Modding tab uses the Nexus Mods orange (`--nexus`); its Nexus total and its two toggles share one row, the total as wide as both toggles (on a phone the toggles show their number and label without the drawing). External action buttons go through `actionLink` in `src/partials/components.mjs`: a platform listed in `data/brands.json` shows its mark ([sources and licences](docs/unify/BRANDS.md)), any other link keeps an arrow icon. The footer signature links to the home on every page. All groups are in the HTML; `src/client/components.js` only shows one tab and one category at a time and handles the menu, the rails, the "Su di me" carousel, the viewer and the deep links. `src/partials/detail-page.mjs` draws the top card of every project page. Use `data/profile.json` for identity and contacts. Keep visible strings and accessible labels paired in `data/i18n/it.json` and `data/i18n/en.json`. The Social Viewer privacy text has a comparison fixture in `docs/unify/privacy-snapshot.txt`.

`data/redirects.json` is active. The seven `/Nexus-portfolio/` source URLs include two aliases for one index file, and `/projects/` is the eighth stub. The build publishes the stubs and copies the assets in `legacy/Nexus-portfolio/assets/` to their original paths. Preserve the exact paths and hashes in `legacy/sha256.json`. Never remove a listed route or file; a target may change. Other project Pages sites use their own repositories and reserved top-level paths. [URL map](docs/unify/URL-MAP.md)

## Commands and release gates

- `node scripts/build.mjs` renders the release into `_site/`. Add `--styleguide` for the QA-only `/styleguide/`. `node scripts/qa/serve.mjs` prints the loopback URL and serves unknown paths with HTTP 404.
- `node scripts/check.mjs` rebuilds, validates all generated HTML and budgets, runs every `scripts/**/test-*.mjs` file and crawls links and assets with `scripts/qa/links.mjs`. CI runs the same command, including the cold-browser check on its installed Chrome.
- `node scripts/qa/audit.mjs --task local` runs Lighthouse and axe on all declared pages, active stubs and the styleguide if built. Each Italian and English run must reach Performance ≥90 mobile and ≥95 desktop (the V3 floors), Accessibility 100 and Best Practices ≥95, with zero axe violations. SEO is recorded. If headless Chrome stalls on the GPU (one unattributed task of a second or more on every page with a picture, typical of a remote-desktop session), run the audit with `QA_CHROME_FLAGS=--disable-gpu`; each row records the flags. For an individual page or home state, run `lighthouse.mjs` or `axe.mjs` in `scripts/qa/` with `--task local --pages /uses/` (axe also takes the home states, e.g. `--pages /#progetti,/#tab-modding`).
- `node scripts/qa/legacy.mjs --task local` follows redirects in Chromium, checks translated links, native refresh and 404 recovery, and verifies every original asset against its SHA-256. Each mapped source and final destination must return HTTP 200.
- `node scripts/qa/i18n-check.mjs --task local` checks both languages at 390 and 1440 px, with every disclosure, tab and category open in turn. Every scanned page and interaction state must have zero language leaks and zero scan errors. Text inside an element whose `lang` names the other language (the arXiv titles) is quoted on purpose and skipped.
- `node scripts/qa/interactions.mjs --task local --pages /` exercises the header menu, the disclosures, tabs, category toggles, rails, certificate fan and viewer, deep links, the no-script page and the live Nexus totals; other pages get the header, target and reflow checks. `node scripts/qa/social-viewer.mjs --task local` checks the product, tester and legal pages. `node scripts/qa/release-layout.mjs --task local` checks every route and home state (`homeStates` in `scripts/qa/browser.mjs`): no horizontal overflow, native image bounds, heading sizes (h1 ≤ 40 px, others ≤ 32 px), 44 px card links, drawing labels of at least 11 px and at most one primary action per view.
- `node scripts/qa/screenshots.mjs --task local --pages /,/#progetti --langs it,en` captures viewport and full-page images at reduced motion; a home fragment opens its section, `/#tab-modding` and `/#tab-teaching` select those tabs and `/#certificazioni` opens the viewer. `node scripts/qa/compare-hero-boxes.mjs --baseline PATH --task local` compares the hero boxes with another built checkout at seven viewport sizes: the title, the CTA, the identity card and the diagram must not move (on phones the card grows by the second line of the role). `node scripts/qa/side-by-side.mjs --task local --reference <prototype>/ref` puts each prototype screenshot beside the matching capture.

Build before browser QA. Use `--task` to select the evidence folder `_review/<task>/`. In Git Bash on Windows, prefix commands that take routes with `MSYS_NO_PATHCONV=1`. Browser tools resolve Playwright, Lighthouse, chrome-launcher, axe-core and Sharp from `~/qa-tools` and read the Chromium executable from `~/qa-tools/chrome-path.txt`. The static budgets, tests and link crawl run with Node alone. With Chromium configured, the validator also checks actual cold home requests at both viewports in both languages.

## Catalogue images

Run `node scripts/tools/make-images.mjs` from the worktree to rebuild the committed WebPs and their dimensions. It reads `~/sources/Nexus-portfolio/assets`, the two Skyrim PNGs in `~/sources/mods` and Sharp from `~/qa-tools` via `createRequire`. CI validates the committed files with Node alone. A second run with identical inputs leaves every output byte intact.

Project and mod covers target 480 and 960 px at quality 80. The hero target is 1440 px for Ringmin, Prof. Gecko, Camaleonte and Social Viewer. The source dimensions after orientation and cropping cap every target. Duplicate widths collapse to one rendition. Covers outside the 5% tolerance are cropped to 16:9. Keep the Social Viewer wordmark in frame with a left-aligned crop. Certificates retain the complete page at every width: thumbnails at 300 and 480 px (the fan's cards are 150 CSS px wide, `sizes="150px"`) and the viewer image at 960 px. After the load event the home starts the thumbnails while the browser is idle, or as soon as the reader points at, focuses or opens the "Chi sono" summary; a card under the pointer or the focus preloads its viewer image. `scripts/qa/home-budget.mjs` counts that idle prefetch apart from the first load (`checkDeferred` in `scripts/budgets.mjs`), and `node scripts/qa/cert-loading.mjs --task <id>` times the fan on the "Fast 4G" profile. and the portrait keeps its square frame. Power-Ringmin shares Ringmin’s diagram. Each catalogue WebP is at most 300,000 bytes and their combined budget is 4,000,000 bytes. The pictures in `assets/nexus/` come from the Nexus refresh, as served (385 px wide): cards never show them larger.

Use `picture(media, { sizes, lang })` or `img(media, options)` from `src/lib/media.mjs`. The helpers read localized alt text and actual image dimensions from the data. Set `fetchpriority: 'high'` only for an image that supplies the page's LCP. Text supplies the home LCP. Inspect the complete catalogue on the QA-only `/styleguide/` after `node scripts/build.mjs --styleguide`. For size-limited review evidence, add `--format webp` to the screenshots command.

## Layout and rendering

Keep the approved hero composition: `src/styles/home.css` starts with the master cascade and ends with the V3 skin, which changes colours, borders and shadows but no box, and the inline `desktop-visual-sizing` block keeps its pinned bytes. The identity ring is an absolutely positioned pseudo-element for that reason. The header stays 64 px tall (63 px bar and 1 px rule). The sections below the hero live in `src/styles/sections.css`. The build inlines up to 14,000 bytes of CSS. Larger pages use shared first-paint styles and one content-addressed stylesheet with a native fallback. Limit the delivered HTML, CSS and JS to 160,000 bytes per page, 260,000 for the home (200,000 of HTML since V5; each published mod adds about 1,050, each certificate about 850), and cold home loads to 450,000 bytes and 15 requests.

Use the escaping `html` template from `src/lib/html.mjs`. Wrap trusted rendered components in `raw()`. Put styles in `src/styles/`, browser scripts in `src/client/`, self-hosted assets in `assets/` and structured data in `data/`. The two original browser scripts retain their public URLs under `scripts/`. Client scripts avoid template literals and block comments so the build can minify them.

The Italian HTML is readable with JavaScript disabled: every tab, category and card is in the page. Use keys from both `data/i18n/` dictionaries in page text, accessible labels and descriptions. The layout embeds the keys used on the route. Keep the `portfolio:languagechange` event for live Nexus totals. The snapshot supplies the initial localized numbers.

## Nexus, Ringmin and release

The scheduled Nexus updater owns `data/nexus-downloads.json`, `data/nexus-mods.json` and `assets/nexus/`. One run of `scripts/update-nexus-downloads.mjs` reads the profile's `uniqueModDownloads` and `modCount`, then every published mod (`mods(filter: uploaderId)`), and downloads the picture of each original mod that has no cover in `data/mods.json`; translations get a typographic header. Never edit those files by hand. Preserve the parser tests (`scripts/test-nexus-downloads.mjs`, `scripts/test-nexus-mods.mjs`) and the snapshot-only commit scope. The public downloads snapshot on `main` is the sole external runtime fetch.

`node scripts/import-ringmin.mjs` copies the certified optima (n = 3…14), the asymptotic table and the certified interval for C* (lower end from Paper II, upper end from the repository's `knowledge/GLOBAL_BOUNDS_ASYMPTOTICS.md`) from `~/sources/ringmin` into `data/ringmin.json`. The Research tab plots R(n)/n² against 1/n with that interval as a band at n = ∞. `scripts/test-ringmin.mjs` checks that every pair of circles in every configuration is tangent or apart.

`Deploy Pages` checks out `main` for pushes to `main`, manual runs and successful completions of `Refresh Nexus Mods downloads` on `main`. The `workflow_run` path also rebuilds Pages when the snapshots are already current.

Work on the task branch in its dedicated worktree and deliver a draft PR to the assigned base. Production deployment belongs to `main`. Keep build output, dependencies and reference clones out of commits. V4 evidence lives in `_review/V4/` (V3 in `_review/V3/`). Use the go-live runbook for the production cutover.
